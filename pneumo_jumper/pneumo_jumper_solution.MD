# Pnuemo Jumper
**Description:** Addition to this year's Haunted Porch.  A simple self standing motion detection triggered prop.  With motion, a relay will fire and trigger a solenoid which will pop an air cylinder having a skeleton "jump" up in a jump scare.  Have always used linear actuators in the past, but these are quite slow.  This should be a little more exciting.

## Components
- Simple motion detector for trigger - triggering off of GPIO 5 / pin 29
